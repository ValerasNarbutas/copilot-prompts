# 🚀 Smart Contract Clause Analyzer & Risk Assessment ⚖️

![Demo of Contract Clause Analyzer](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt analyzes legal contracts to identify key clauses, potential risks, unfavorable terms, and compliance issues. It helps legal teams, procurement, and business leaders understand contract implications before signing.

## Prompt 💡

Analyze the following contract document and provide a comprehensive analysis including:
- **Contract summary**: Key parties, effective dates, contract value, and term length
- **Critical clauses identification**:
  - Payment terms and conditions
  - Termination clauses and notice periods
  - Liability and indemnification provisions
  - Intellectual property rights
  - Confidentiality and non-disclosure terms
  - Dispute resolution mechanisms
  - Non-compete and exclusivity clauses
- **Risk assessment**: Flag high-risk provisions with severity ratings
- **Unfavorable terms**: Clauses that may disadvantage our organization
- **Missing protections**: Standard clauses that should be included
- **Compliance check**: Regulatory and legal compliance verification
- **Negotiation points**: Suggested amendments with justification
- **Comparison to standards**: How terms compare to industry norms
- **Financial implications**: Total cost of ownership and hidden costs
- **Action items**: Steps required before signing

Format the output with risk ratings (High/Medium/Low), prioritized recommendations, and an executive summary for decision-makers.

Contract document:
{Paste_Contract_Text_Here}

Contract type: {Specify_Contract_Type}
(e.g., Vendor Agreement, Service Contract, Employment Agreement, NDA, Lease Agreement)

## Description ℹ️

This prompt helps organizations understand complex legal contracts by extracting key terms, identifying risks, and providing actionable recommendations. It accelerates contract review, reduces legal costs, and helps prevent unfavorable agreements.
