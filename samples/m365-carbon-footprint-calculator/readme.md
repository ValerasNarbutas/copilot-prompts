# 🚀 Business Carbon Footprint Calculator & Sustainability Planner 🌱

![Demo of Carbon Footprint Calculator](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt helps organizations calculate their carbon footprint and create actionable sustainability plans. It analyzes business operations, travel, energy consumption, and supply chain to provide emissions estimates and reduction strategies aligned with ESG goals.

## Prompt 💡

Analyze the following business operations data and create a comprehensive carbon footprint report including:
- **Total CO2 emissions estimate** (in metric tons) broken down by category:
  - Office energy consumption
  - Employee commuting and business travel
  - Data center and cloud computing
  - Supply chain and logistics
  - Manufacturing/production (if applicable)
- **Emissions comparison** to industry benchmarks
- **Carbon intensity metrics** (emissions per employee, per revenue dollar)
- **Top 5 emission sources** with percentage contribution
- **Reduction opportunities** with estimated impact and implementation cost
- **Sustainability roadmap** with short-term (0-6 months) and long-term (1-3 years) goals
- **ROI analysis** for each reduction initiative
- **Progress tracking metrics** and KPIs

Format the output with charts descriptions, tables, and executive summary suitable for ESG reporting.

Business operations data:
{Paste_Your_Operations_Data_Here}

Include details on:
- Number of employees and office locations
- Annual energy consumption (kWh)
- Travel data (flights, miles driven)
- Data center/cloud usage
- Supply chain information

## Description ℹ️

This prompt helps businesses measure and reduce their environmental impact by calculating carbon emissions and providing data-driven sustainability strategies. Perfect for ESG reporting, sustainability initiatives, and achieving net-zero targets.

## Instructions 📝

1. Make sure you have Copilot for Microsoft 365 in your tenant
2. Open the Microsoft Teams app or Copilot in your browser
3. Open the Copilot app within Teams
4. Paste the prompt in the Copilot app
5. Replace {Paste_Your_Operations_Data_Here} with your business operations data

### Improvise Usage 🚀

1. Compare carbon footprint year-over-year
2. Calculate carbon offset costs for neutrality
3. Request specific reduction strategies (renewable energy, remote work policies)
4. Generate carbon credit purchase recommendations
5. Create employee engagement initiatives for sustainability
6. Benchmark against competitors or industry leaders
