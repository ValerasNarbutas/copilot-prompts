# 🚀 Team Skills Gap Analysis & Development Planner 📚

![Demo of Skills Gap Analyzer](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt analyzes team skills against project requirements and industry standards to identify skill gaps, create personalized development plans, and recommend training resources. Ideal for HR, team leads, and professional development planning.

## Prompt 💡

Analyze the team skills data below and create a comprehensive skills gap analysis report including:
- **Current skills inventory**: Team-wide skills matrix with proficiency levels
- **Required skills analysis**: Skills needed for upcoming projects and strategic goals
- **Skills gap identification**: Critical gaps prioritized by business impact
- **Individual development plans**: Personalized learning paths for each team member
- **Training recommendations**: 
  - Online courses and certifications
  - Mentorship opportunities
  - Hands-on project assignments
  - External training programs
- **Timeline and milestones**: Realistic skill development roadmap (3-12 months)
- **Budget estimate**: Approximate costs for training and development
- **ROI projection**: Expected impact on team productivity and project success
- **Succession planning**: Identifying future leaders and critical skill dependencies
- **Market competitiveness**: How team skills compare to industry standards

Format the output with visual skill matrices, prioritized action items, and executive summary.

Include:
- Current team member skills and experience levels
- Upcoming project requirements
- Strategic business objectives
- Budget constraints (if any)

## Description ℹ️

This prompt helps organizations identify skill gaps, create targeted development plans, and ensure teams have the capabilities needed for current and future success. It supports strategic workforce planning and employee development initiatives.

## Instructions 📝

1. Make sure you have Copilot for Microsoft 365 in your tenant
2. Open the Microsoft Teams app
3. Open the Copilot app within Teams
4. Paste the prompt in the Copilot app
5. Replace {Paste_Team_Skills_Data_Here} with your team information

### Improvise Usage 🚀

1. Compare skills against specific job market demands
2. Request skills mapping to organizational competency frameworks
3. Generate team composition recommendations for new projects
4. Identify cross-training opportunities within the team
5. Create skills-based team reorganization proposals
6. Track skill development progress over time
7. Benchmark against competitors or industry leaders
