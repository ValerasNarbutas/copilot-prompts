# 🚀 Meeting Decisions and Follow-ups Tracker 🎯

![Demo of Meeting Decision Tracker](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt extracts and organizes all decisions made, action items assigned, and follow-ups required from meeting transcripts or notes. It creates a structured tracker to ensure accountability and progress monitoring.

## Prompt 💡

Analyze the following meeting transcript/notes and create a comprehensive decision and action tracker including:
- **Decisions Made**: List all decisions with decision owner and date
- **Action Items**: Extract tasks with assignee, due date, and priority
- **Follow-up Required**: Items requiring clarification or future discussion
- **Key Commitments**: Commitments made by team members with deadlines
- **Parking Lot Items**: Topics deferred for later discussion
- **Dependencies**: Cross-team dependencies identified
- **Risk Flags**: Decisions or actions that may have risks or blockers

Format the output as organized tables with status tracking columns. Include a summary section highlighting urgent items and next meeting agenda topics.

Meeting content:
{Paste_Meeting_Transcript_or_Notes_Here}

Meeting date: {Specify_Date}

## Description ℹ️

This prompt transforms meeting discussions into actionable, trackable items. It ensures nothing falls through the cracks by systematically extracting decisions, actions, and commitments, making meeting outcomes clear and measurable.

## Instructions 📝

1. Make sure you have Copilot for Microsoft 365 in your tenant
2. Open the Microsoft Teams app
3. Open the Copilot app within Teams
4. Paste the prompt in the Copilot app
5. Replace {Paste_Meeting_Transcript_or_Notes_Here} with your meeting content
6. Specify the meeting date
