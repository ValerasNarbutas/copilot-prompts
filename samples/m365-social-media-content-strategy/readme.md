# 🚀 AI-Powered Social Media Content Strategy Generator 📱

![Demo of Social Media Content Strategy](./assets/demo.png)

## Summary

This prompt creates comprehensive social media content strategies tailored to your brand, audience, and business goals. It generates content calendars, post ideas, engagement tactics, and performance metrics aligned with current social media trends.

## Prompt 💡

Create a comprehensive 30-day social media content strategy for the following business including:
- **Platform-specific strategies**: Tailored approaches for each platform (LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- **Content calendar**: 30-day posting schedule with:
  - Daily post ideas with captions
  - Optimal posting times
  - Content themes and pillars
  - Mix of content types (educational, promotional, engaging, behind-the-scenes)
- **Content formats**: 
  - Short-form videos
  - Carousel posts
  - Infographics
  - Polls and interactive content
  - User-generated content campaigns
  - Live sessions/webinars
- **Trending topics integration**: Current trends and hashtags relevant to your industry
- **Engagement tactics**: Strategies to boost likes, comments, shares, and followers
- **Influencer collaboration ideas**: Potential partnerships and co-marketing opportunities
- **Paid advertising recommendations**: Budget allocation and ad campaign ideas
- **KPIs and metrics**: Success metrics to track for each platform
- **Crisis management**: Guidelines for handling negative feedback
- **Content repurposing matrix**: How to adapt content across platforms

Format the output as a structured content calendar with actionable daily tasks.

Business information:
{Paste_Business_Details_Here}

Include:
- Industry and target audience
- Brand voice and values
- Current social media presence
- Marketing goals (awareness, leads, sales, engagement)
- Budget constraints
- Competitor accounts to monitor

## Description ℹ️

This prompt helps marketers and business owners create data-driven social media strategies that increase engagement, grow followers, and drive business results. It combines best practices with current trends to create authentic, engaging content.

## Instructions 📝

1. Make sure you have Copilot for Microsoft 365 in your tenant
2. Open the Microsoft Teams app or Copilot in your browser
3. Open the Copilot app within Teams
4. Paste the prompt in the Copilot app
5. Replace {Paste_Business_Details_Here} with your business information

### Improvise Usage 🚀

1. Request seasonal or holiday-specific content ideas
2. Ask for A/B testing recommendations for posts
3. Generate specific campaign ideas (product launch, event promotion)
4. Request competitor analysis and differentiation strategy
5. Create crisis communication plan templates
6. Generate employee advocacy program content
7. Design viral content campaigns
8. Request analytics dashboard setup recommendations
